Room Renovation Plan

Supplies: gloves, dust mask, goggles, pry bar, mallet, utility knife, saw, miter box, tape measure, pencil, cleaning supplies, wall repair kit, paint, primer, brushes, rollers, trays, masking tape, drop cloth, laminate flooring, underlayment, spacers, baseboards, glue, waste disposal service

1. Preparation
1.1. Move all belongings to another room.
1.2. Disassemble and move all furniture.
1.3. Turn off power and remove wall plates.
1.4. Remove the baseboards, floor, and underlayment.
1.5. Vacuum the subfloor.

2. Painting
2.1. Clean and patch the walls.
2.2. Protect window/door frames.
2.3. Paint the walls and wait 3 hours.
2.4. Apply a second coat and wait 24 hours.

3. Flooring
3.1. Place new underlayment and floor boards.
3.2. Measure and cut new baseboards.
3.3. Glue baseboards to the walls.

4. Wrap-up
4.1. Dispose of the waste according to the regulations.
4.2. Reinstall wall plates.
4.3. Bring back the furniture and belongings.
